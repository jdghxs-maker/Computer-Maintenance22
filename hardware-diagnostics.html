Hardware Diagnostics & Repair

We test components, replace failing parts, and clear out the dust and heat that quietly kill machines. If it's a physical problem, this is where it gets fixed.

> Component testing — RAM, PSU, storage, GPU
> Part replacement and upgrades
> Dust removal and thermal repaste
> Boot and POST failure troubleshooting
> Cable and connection checks
> Full build diagnostics
01

Intake & symptom check

We log what's wrong — won't boot, overheats, random shutdowns — before opening the case.

02

Component testing

Each part is tested in isolation to find the actual point of failure.

03

Repair or replace

We fix, reseat, clean, or swap the failing part — whichever gets it running again.

04

Stress test & sign-off

The machine runs under load before it's marked complete.
