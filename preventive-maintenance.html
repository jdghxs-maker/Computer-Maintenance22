Preventive Maintenance Plans

Most repairs are cheaper than the failure they prevent. We run scheduled check-ups so small issues get caught before they become downtime.

> Scheduled hardware check-ups
> Automated and manual backups
> System health reports
> Dust and thermal maintenance on a schedule
> Update and patch scheduling
> Early warning on failing components
01

Set the schedule

We agree on a check-up interval based on how the machine is used.

02

Run the check-up

Hardware health, backup status, and pending updates are all reviewed.

03

Report findings

We flag anything trending toward failure before it causes downtime.

04

Act early

Small fixes now instead of emergency repairs later.
